Policy

Larsen & Toubro Transportation Infrastructure IC is committed to planning, deploying, operating, maintaining, refurbishing, and retiring assets across their lifecycle in a disciplined and sustainable way.

  • Compliance with legal, environmental, contractual, and regulatory requirements.
  • Integration with safety, quality, and environmental management systems.
  • Reliable asset data to improve decisions, service quality, and performance review.

Department Role

The Plant and Machinery team keeps construction operations supported by dependable equipment, responsive maintenance, and consistent readiness across sites and packages.

  • Reduce downtime through disciplined monitoring and quicker reporting.
  • Support project teams with plant, machinery, and operational visibility.
  • Drive continuous improvement through digital records and shared learning.
